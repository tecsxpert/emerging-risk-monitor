## Day 16 — Demo Scenarios

### 1. Create Risk
**Steps:**
- Go to List Page
- Click "Create Risk"
- Enter name and select level
- Click Submit

**Expected Output:**
- New risk appears in the list

---

### 2. View Risk Detail
**Steps:**
- Go to List Page
- Click on a risk name

**Expected Output:**
- Detail page opens with full information

---

### 3. Edit Risk
**Steps:**
- Open Detail Page
- Click Edit
- Update the data
- Click Save

**Expected Output:**
- Updated data is reflected in the list

---

### 4. Delete Risk
**Steps:**
- Open Detail Page
- Click Delete

**Expected Output:**
- Risk is removed from the list

---

### 5. Dashboard & Analytics
**Steps:**
- Open Dashboard
- View stats
- Navigate to Analytics page

**Expected Output:**
- Charts display risk distribution correctly
